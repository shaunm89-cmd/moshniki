Frequently asked questions

Have a quick look below before asking for support — your question may already be answered.

Why is my stream buffering? +

Buffering can happen for a number of reasons and doesn't always mean there's an issue with the service. Common causes include:

  • Slow or unstable internet
  • Weak Wi-Fi signal
  • Internet congestion during busy periods
  • Device running low on memory or storage
  • App or device needing a restart
  • Temporary issue with a particular channel

Try this first:

  • Restart your streaming device
  • Restart your modem/router
  • Try a few different channels
  • Use Ethernet instead of Wi-Fi if possible
  • Clear the app cache
  • Check Discord for any known issues

If only one channel is buffering and everything else works normally, please report that channel.

One channel isn't working — what do I do? +

Try a few other channels first. If everything else is working, report the affected channel and include:

  • Channel name
  • Device / app you're using
  • What is happening
  • VPN — yes or no?
Nothing is working — where do I start? +

If no channels are working:

  • Check your subscription is active
  • Check your internet connection
  • Close and reopen the app
  • Restart your device
  • Restart your modem/router
  • Check Discord for service announcements

Still not working? Contact a moderator.

Wi-Fi or Ethernet — which should I use? +

Ethernet is recommended whenever possible. A wired connection is generally more stable and isn't affected by Wi-Fi interference or weak signal strength.

Should I clear my app cache? +

Yes. If your app is slow, freezing or behaving strangely, clearing the cache can help.

Do not clear data unless you know your login/setup information, as this can reset the app.

Where do I find my username & password? +

Check the Help Guide — we have tutorials showing how to find your login details on supported Austreams apps and devices.

How do I download Austreams apps? +

Install Downloader and enter code 234753. Install and open Unlinked, then enter library code B46DA2E8. Choose the app you need and install it. Full walkthrough on the Install Guide page.

Should I restart my device? +

Yes! It sounds simple, but restarting fixes a surprising number of issues. If your device stays powered on all the time, give it a restart occasionally.

Where do I find live sport / special events? +

Check the Austreams Sport Announcements section and keep an eye on Discord for the latest event schedules. Major sporting events may have dedicated Special Event channels — check Live Pay-Per-View.

Why hasn't a sporting event started yet? +

Broadcast times can change and some feeds may not begin until shortly before the event starts. Check the latest schedule posted in Discord before reporting it.

Is there a service issue right now? +

Check the Service Status / Announcements section of Discord. If we're aware of a widespread issue, updates will be posted there when available.

Still need help? +

If you've tried the steps above and still need help, contact us through the Support channel and tell us:

  • Channel / App
  • Device
  • Problem
  • When it started

The more information you give us, the easier it is for us to help.

Didn't find your answer?

Head into the Support channel on Discord and a moderator will help you out.
