About me

Warm, approachable, and grounded in care.

I am a warm and approachable psychologist dedicated to supporting the emotional wellbeing of children, young people, and their families. My work is grounded in a person-centred approach, and I strive to create a therapeutic environment where clients feel safe, understood, and supported.

I tailor each session to the individual, recognising the unique experiences, strengths, and needs that every young person brings. My career has taken me across a range of clinical and educational settings, including work with university students and within primary and secondary schools.

These experiences have deepened my understanding of child and adolescent development, learning needs, academic pressures, and the emotional hurdles young people and families may encounter.

Meaningful therapy begins when people feel safe enough to be themselves.
Individual therapy

Support for life’s heavy moments.

I offer a calm, supportive space where you can talk openly about what has been feeling heavy, overwhelming, or hard to put into words. Whether you are seeking support for yourself or your child, you do not have to navigate it alone.

Who I support

I support the emotional wellbeing of children and adolescents aged 5–18, university students, adults, and families.

I work with concerns including anxiety, academic stress, emotional regulation, grief, self-esteem, low mood, stress, emotional overwhelm, and life transitions.

I also have a particular interest in supporting women during the perinatal period, including postnatal anxiety, postnatal depression, matrescence, and adjustment to parenthood.

First session

What to expect when beginning.

The first session focuses on understanding the presenting concerns and gathering important developmental, family, and background information to guide treatment planning.

1

Adults

Adult clients attend an initial 60-minute appointment to discuss history, strengths, current concerns, and hopes for therapy.

2

Children under 14

The initial appointment is held with parents or caregivers only to discuss the child’s history, strengths, and areas of concern.

3

Young people 14+

The session is generally divided into 30 minutes with the young person and 30 minutes with parents or caregivers.
